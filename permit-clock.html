How the clock works

  • Wait is the day a permit was issued minus the day it was filed, in calendar days, so weekends and holidays count.
  • Permits issued the same day they were filed are counted separately. Mixing a same-day approval with a two-month plan review would hide what both groups go through.
  • Typical is the median: the middle permit when every wait is lined up. One permit stuck for a year can drag an average way up, but it barely moves the middle.
  • Months are grouped by when permits were issued. Slow permits still in review would otherwise make recent months look faster than they are; they show up under “Still waiting now” instead.
  • The clock includes days an applicant spent fixing plans or answering questions, so not every day is the county’s.
  • Real numbers would come from dataMontgomery’s Residential Permit dataset (m88u-pqki), using its addeddate and issueddate fields. This prototype uses made-up permits in that same shape.
